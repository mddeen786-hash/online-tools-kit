Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our free online PDF and image tools.

Is Filesque completely free to use? +
Yes, every tool on Filesque is 100% free. No account registration, no login, and no payment required. Simply visit the tool and start working.
How do I convert a PDF to Word online? +
Open the PDF to Word tool, upload your PDF, and click Convert. Your editable Word document downloads automatically within seconds.
How can I merge multiple PDF files into one? +
Use the Merge PDF tool. Upload two or more PDFs, drag to reorder if needed, then click Merge. Your combined PDF downloads instantly.
Are my uploaded files safe and private? +
Yes. All files are processed securely and automatically deleted from the server after your download. We never store, read, or share your documents.
Can I compress a PDF without losing quality? +
Yes. The Compress PDF tool reduces file size while keeping text and images readable — perfect for email attachments and document sharing.
What image formats does the Image Converter support? +
The Image Converter supports JPG, JPEG, PNG, WEBP, BMP, and ICO formats. Upload your image, choose the output format, and download instantly.
Does the PDF to Image tool convert every page? +
Yes. The PDF to Image tool converts all pages of your PDF into high-resolution JPG images and packages them in a ZIP file for easy download.
Do these tools work on mobile phones? +
Yes. All Filesque tools are fully mobile-friendly and work on Android phones, iPhones, tablets, and desktop browsers without needing any app installation.
